Start Using Bougainville

Bougainville is a keyboard for Rapoisi, Halia, Tinputz, Terei, Sibe, Petats and Naasioi (Bougainville, Papua New Guinea), with extra characters for phonology work. Most special letters are typed with ; followed by a letter. For capitals, : (Shift+;) works as well as ;.

The keys ; ` ~ q and x wait for the next key: nothing appears until you type it. If the next key isn't part of a sequence, both characters are typed, so ; then a space still gives a semicolon. To cancel, press Backspace.

WhatsApp and Similar Apps

Some apps, such as WhatsApp, don't let a keyboard change a character after it has been typed. In those apps, everything typed with ; ` ~ q or x, and the Right Alt keys for quotes and dashes, works normally. These older sequences change a character already typed, so they may not work there: : then a capital, the macron vowels (a=, a=a), the quote and dash sequences ('', "", --, Right Alt+Shift+< then <), and the second and later steps of the IPA sequences (such as qaa). Use ; for capitals and the Right Alt keys for quotes and dashes in those apps.

Phones and Tablets

On touch devices, press and hold a letter to choose its special forms: a ä ā á ą ɑ α æ, e ë ē é ę ɛ ə, i ï ī í į ɩ ı ɨ, o ö ō ó ǫ ɔ, u ü ū ú ų υ ʊ, n ŋ, c ɔ, v ʌ, g ɡ ɣ, s σ ş ṣ, r ɾ, b β, p φ. Capitals are on the Shift layer.

Press and hold . for the glottals ʼ ꞌ ʔ, the curly quotes ‘ ’ “ ”, the ` and ~ keys for accented and nasalized vowels, and other punctuation. On the 123 layer, press and hold - for – — and the non-breaking and soft hyphens. For double-macron vowels, choose ā (or ē, ī, ō, ū) and then type the same vowel again. All the typing sequences above also work on touch devices.
